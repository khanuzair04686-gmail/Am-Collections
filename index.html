What exactly is a 1:1 Master Copy Watch? ▼

A 1:1 Master Copy (or super clone) is manufactured using genuine 904L stainless steel, authentic ceramic bezels, scratch-resistant sapphire coated crystals, and Japanese mechanical or quartz movements. It mirrors the exact millimeter dimensions, weight, dial typography, and clasp mechanisms of the original luxury reference.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) and delivery verification work? ▼

Fill in your delivery details on our Cash on Delivery checkout form or confirm on WhatsApp. Our express courier partner (BlueDart / Delhivery / DTDC) will deliver to your doorstep within 2 to 4 business days. How does the 'Order via WhatsApp' button function? ▼

Clicking the WhatsApp button automatically formats your cart items, model specifications, total price, and shipping details into a pre-filled WhatsApp message sent straight to our dedicated store concierge number. We verify your order in minutes, share dispatch tracking details, and answer any custom questions directly on chat.

Do these watches have a smooth sweeping second hand? ▼

Yes! All our automatic models (Rolex Submariner, Daytona, Datejust, Patek Nautilus, AP Royal Oak) are powered by high-beat Japanese automatic calibers with a smooth sweeping second hand without any quartz tick-tock stutter.

What is your replacement policy? ▼

We provide a 7-day hassle-free replacement guarantee. If your watch has any transit damage, sizing issue, or mechanical defect, message us on WhatsApp and we will dispatch a brand-new replacement immediately with doorstep reverse pickup.
